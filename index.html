Active asking prices are not confirmed sold prices. Flip Finder labels estimates clearly and opens marketplace searches so you can verify details before buying.

Asking prices vs. sold prices

Marketplace search results can show current asking prices, which are not proof an item sold for that amount. Use completed/sold listings when a marketplace provides them, compare like-for-like condition and model, and account for fees, shipping, returns, and taxes.

How the estimate works

When a market estimate is available, the Buy/Sell tools use it as a starting point. An estimate is not a guarantee of sale price or profit. Confirm the listing details and your actual costs before purchasing.
